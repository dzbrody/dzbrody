## What I do

Engagements are scoped to a stage and a financial objective. The work below is how that shows up in the codebase and the platform.

- **Product commercialization.** Turn IP and architecture into a market-ready product: roadmap, build leadership, go-to-market alignment, and investor-ready technical documentation.
- **Technical due diligence.** Independent read of architecture, team, debt, and risk for investors, acquirers, and boards working against a deadline.
- **Platform stabilization.** Reliability, infrastructure cost, and delivery speed when a platform is already under stress.
- **Exit and M&A preparation.** Pre-transaction hardening, the technical narrative for the investment memo, integration planning, and CTO transition.
- **Engineering leadership.** Interim CTO, VP Engineering, or board advisor: delivery standards, hiring, and cadence for a high-growth team.
- **AI and platform modernization.** Cloud transformation on AWS, Azure, and GCP, AI-enabled ERP, enterprise data architecture, and security governance in regulated environments.
